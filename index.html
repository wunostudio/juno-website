Common questions

How do I scan?

Open the scanner and point the camera at the shelf. Hold steady in decent light. A barcode is the most reliable read. Spine text is used when a barcode is not in view.

What is a hit?

A hit is a book at or above the minimum resale price you set. You can also require a sales rank. Lower rank means a more popular book. Turn rank off to use price only.

Are the prices exact?

No. They are market estimates. Condition, edition, and demand change what a book actually sells for. Use the eBay or Amazon link to check current listings.

Does it work offline?

The camera can read a barcode or spine on the device, but identifying the book and loading a price needs an internet connection.

I subscribed, but the app still shows the paywall.

Stay signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe, then tap Restore Purchases. Signing in alone does not attach a subscription. If you deleted your Juno account and made a new one, Restore is what attaches an Apple subscription that is still active.

How do I cancel?

Apple bills the subscription, so cancel it in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Deleting your Juno account does not stop that billing.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete Account. That removes the Juno account and the data described in the Privacy Policy. It does not cancel the Apple subscription.

Is the camera recorded?

No. Frames stay on the iPhone long enough to read a barcode or spine. We do not upload photos or video. The title, author, or ISBN from that read is sent so we can look up the book.
