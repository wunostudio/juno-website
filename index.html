What Juno does

Built for scanning shelves at thrift stores, library sales and estate sales.

📷

Barcode and spine, together

Juno reads ISBN barcodes and spine text from the same camera view, at the same time. There's no mode to switch, and no need to pull every book off the shelf.

Markers on the shelf

Small green and purple markers appear in AR on the spines Juno matches. They're colour only, with no text on them, so the shelf stays easy to read.

🔖

Watchlist

Save keywords and authors you're looking for. When one turns up on a spine, it gets a purple marker.

📖

Barcode result card

Scan a book's barcode and a card with its title and author appears at the bottom of the screen. Markers aren't tappable, so the barcode is the way to get details.

🔗

Listing check

From the result card, open an Amazon or eBay search for the book's ISBN and see what's listed right now.

⚙️

Your settings, saved

Sign in with Apple. Your watchlist and scan settings are saved to your Juno account.

Inside the app

How it works

1

Sign in

Sign in with Apple, then subscribe or start the 7-day trial if Apple offers it on your Apple ID.

2

Add your watchlist

In Settings, add the keywords and authors you're hunting for.

3

Point at a shelf

Move slowly along the shelf in good light. Juno reads barcodes and spine text together, and markers appear on the spines it matches.

4

Scan for details

Scan a book's barcode to see its title and author, then open the Amazon or eBay search before you decide.

Support

            

                
Common questions

                

                    
How do I scan?

                    
Open the scanner and point the camera at the shelf in good light, moving slowly. Juno reads barcodes and spine text at the same time. To see a book's details, scan its barcode.

                

                

                    
What do the green and purple markers mean?

                    
Purple marks a keyword or author from your watchlist. Green marks a book that matches your scan settings. Markers are colour only and can't be tapped; scan the barcode for details.

                

                

                    
Does it work offline?

                    
No. Juno needs an internet connection to identify books.

                

                

                    
I subscribed, but the app still shows the paywall.

                    
Stay signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe, then tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen. Signing in alone does not attach a subscription.

                

                

                    
How do I cancel?

                    
Apple bills the subscription, so cancel it in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Deleting your Juno account does not stop that billing.

                

                

                    
How do I delete my account?

                    
In the app, open Settings and tap Delete Account. That removes the Juno account and the data described in the Privacy Policy. It does not cancel the Apple subscription.

                

                

                    
Is the camera recorded?

                    
No. Juno reads barcodes and spine text on your iPhone and doesn't upload photos or video. Only the text it reads, such as a title or ISBN, is sent so we can identify the book.
